These images are original, non-shipping visual references generated for the
Milestone 2 workshop with the built-in image-generation path. They are not logo,
UI, plot, or production assets.

## Circadian Field

File: `public/mood-circadian-field.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: non-shipping brand moodboard atmosphere
> for a scientific web application. Create an original abstract atmosphere for
> “Circadian Field”: a calm deep blue-green field transitioning toward a pale
> daylight horizon, with one continuous luminous cyan arc suggesting a 24-hour
> circadian cycle, restrained translucent time bands, and a small amber horizon
> glow. Refined matte editorial abstraction, subtle paper-like microtexture,
> minimal and spacious, scientifically credible. Wide landscape composition.
> No text, letters, logos, UI, charts, people, devices, literal sun or moon,
> hexagon, 3D badge, or watermark. Avoid neon cyberpunk, rainbow spectrum,
> glossy rendering, stock-photo styling, and clutter.

## Clear Measure

File: `public/mood-clear-measure.png`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: non-shipping brand moodboard atmosphere
> for a scientific web application. Primary request: Create an original abstract
> atmosphere for “Clear Measure”. Scene/backdrop: a nearly white and pale
> cool-gray architectural field with controlled negative space and quiet daylight
> diffusion. Subject: one precise deep cobalt horizontal light band, a few
> hairline slate alignment rules, and subtle matte paper-and-mineral microtexture.
> Style/medium: modernist abstract brand atmosphere; restrained, exact, minimal,
> spacious, and highly professional. Composition/framing: wide landscape
> composition with a disciplined asymmetric grid and generous open space. Color
> palette: off-white, pale cool gray, slate, and one deep cobalt accent.
> Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, charts, people, devices,
> literal sun or moon, hexagon, 3D badge, glassmorphism, glossy rendering,
> watermark, or clutter. Avoid: corporate-stock gradients, rainbow color,
> decorative flourishes, futuristic HUD styling, photorealistic office interiors,
> and visual noise.
